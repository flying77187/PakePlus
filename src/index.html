How to Play

  • Use arrow keys to control the car: Up to accelerate, Down to brake, Left/Right to steer.
  • Navigate from the START line to the FINISH line as quickly as possible.
  • Avoid the red obstacles - hitting them will slow you down!
  • Press SPACE for emergency brake to stop immediately.
  • Press R to reset the game at any time.
  • Press P to pause or resume the game.
  • Try to complete the track in the shortest time possible!
